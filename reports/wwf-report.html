About this research

Inside the report

This report was prepared during an internship with WWF-India's Climate and Energy Division. It explores the evolution of National Adaptation Plans, India's adaptation policy framework, international experiences and opportunities to strengthen adaptation planning.

01 · POLICY FRAMEWORK

Evolution of National Adaptation Plans

Examines the development of NAPs, the Paris Agreement, the Global Goal on Adaptation and the international policy context for adaptation.

02 · INDIA

India's adaptation landscape

Reviews climate vulnerability, national policy priorities, sectoral adaptation needs and barriers to implementation.

03 · COMPARATIVE RESEARCH

International experiences

Compares national adaptation planning approaches in South Africa, Brazil, Pakistan and Vietnam.

04 · RECOMMENDATIONS

Building climate resilience

Highlights local action plans, Traditional Ecological Knowledge, inclusive data, climate finance, institutional capacity and Nature-based Solutions.
